## Reddit post

**Title:** I built an addon for Forever that translates foreign-language chat right in the chat frame, offline, 17 languages

**Body:**

On the EU realms General chat in the starting zones is half French, Dutch or German, so I wrote an addon that appends the English meaning to the line itself, inside the chat frame. Screenshot is real General chat in Dun Morogh this morning.

It needs nothing else: no account, no service, no program running next to the game. The addon carries its own dictionaries and grammar for 17 languages (Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, German, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Chinese, Korean, Japanese) and works the meaning out inside the game, so nothing leaves your PC.

Being honest about what it is: this is not Google Translate. The output is often fluent, sometimes clumsy and literal, now and then wrong. The goal is that a guild advert, a trade offer or a joke in a language you don't read is no longer noise. When it understands too little of a line it says nothing rather than guess.

Cost: loads in under a second at login, about 36 MB, about a millisecond per new chat line, nothing per frame.

Known limits: the client hides chat from addons in dungeons, raids and battlegrounds, so nothing is translated there. Names and item names stay as they are.

CurseForge: {URL}

Every release improves the dictionaries from real chat, so the most useful thing you can do is paste a line it got wrong and what it should have said. If there's a language you'd want added, say which.
